## 1. Checks that claimed more than they checked

### 1.1 The countdown's own number read as ungrounded (#1963, #1964)

`core/facts/date_math.py` measured every "N days before" back from today and dropped every future
fact date, so "Sixteen days before launch" could only be compared with a Nov 1 snippet saying "18
days" - flagged, and the hold reason was "it names something the facts do not". The countdown's
own line ("16 days from November 3") never counted. A held countdown day was then lost: the
day's run existed, so `series_drafted` skipped it, though the operator had rejected the draft at
17:08. Day 18 (run 221) was lost the same way on Nov 1, to a similarity hold. Fixed: "before"
counts forward to a future date; a rejected draft or a retired render no longer holds its day; a
countdown past its day is dead, not waiting.

### 1.2 The machine's own directions were graded as the operator's idea (#1967)

For every overnight draft that was not a preview, `core/batch_generation.py` joined the script
experiment's arm, the preview shape and the follow line into `creative_brief`, which the engine
labels "THE OPERATOR'S IDEA", checks with `_keep_to_idea` (a premium rewrite on "no") and counts
as the operator's own points. Wave 144 put the follow line on most drafts; tonight 2 of 5 logged
"script may not answer the operator's idea: why to follow ...". The directions now travel as the
pipeline's existing style directive; the idea is the operator's words or a preview's call.

### 1.3 S15 counted one night three times (#1968)

`menu_mix` read one night's three-row menu once for each of its drafts, and overnight's slate -
the series, one preview, then headlines past the three-outlet gate - never saw wave 141's gaming
floor, which lives in the interactive menu. Gaming was one row in three every night while it
earned 62% of organic views (7,235 of 11,605 in the synced windows). A menu is counted once now,
and the night as a whole carries the menu's gaming share.

### 1.4 Silence from a model passed as "fine"

The cheap tier's OpenRouter default (`poolside/laguna-s-2.1:free`) had been retired: every night
began with a 404 and a 24-hour skip. Of the 14 free models listed tonight, one answered cleanly -
and returned nothing at all within 20-120 tokens about half the time. The second read and every
cheap gate read "" as no objection, so an empty answer would have quietly passed renders. An empty
answer now fails over to the next provider, and the default is the model that answered.

### 1.5 A guard that could not fail

`core/ops_doc_verbs.verbs_named_in_docs` (#633) existed so docs could not name a removed command;
its only test asserted that one command was registered. The living docs are now checked against
the registry (0 stale today), with a fixture that proves the check fails.

### 1.6 The record misses most of the paid views

YouTube counts 44,114 paid Shorts views in 90 days (4,970 on 10-27). The publish log records 1,874
paid views, all on run 196. Run 170 - the Modern Warfare 4 Short the $14.55 campaign promoted -
has 37,602 lifetime views, 325 in its synced window and 0 recorded as paid: the campaign's views
came after its window. Anything that reads lifetime views reads them as organic (#1977).

### 1.7 CI, and the last audit's guards

90 runs since `113b46a`: 85 green, 3 red (each fixed within 5-85 minutes), 2 cancelled; the
longest red streak is 1 run (the last audit found 22). `tests/test_grand_audit.py` still runs
the bad import order in a child process; the suite still refuses a bare run.
