## Checkpoints

Each wave is judged **14 days after it ships**, on its lane's number (B10.15, the wave scorecard):
the target met keeps its direction; missed twice, its lane's next wave changes approach or the
lane gives its share to the others (B10.5).

- **Dec 1 - the GTA 6 window.** B11.25: GTA 6 organic views and long-form hours from Oct 28 to Nov
  30 against every other subject. If GTA long-form earned hours, the long-form kind becomes the
  weekly default for the marquee games (B06.2, B06.3); if it did not, long-form returns to the
  weekly Week in Review only.
- **Jan 5 - mid-race.** The four numbers against the table above. Each lane at under half its
  target for four weeks gives its wave share to the lane that is ahead.
- **Jan 31 - before the reported bar change.** The honest status written in the planning log: the
  numbers, what moved them, and what the new bar (B10.2, once confirmed - B10.16) means for the
  next phase.

## How this page stays true

The targets are revised only at a checkpoint, with the reason in the planning log; a week's actual
numbers come from the scoreboard (wave 134) and are not typed by hand.
