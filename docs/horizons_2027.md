# TapIn — horizons to 2027 and after

> **Class:** plan · **Status:** living · **Reviewed:** 2026-10-30

Where TapIn goes after the bridge: the horizons, the signposts that say whether it is on course,
the branches when it is not, and the conditions that should make the operator stop and ask. Until
2027-02-01 the plan is the bridge ([bridge_plan.md](bridge_plan.md), week by week in
[bridge_calendar.md](bridge_calendar.md)), and nothing here overrides it. The engine's own forward
plan is [master_plan.md](master_plan.md). This page picks up where both stop and does not repeat
them. Why the channel exists is [tapin_philosophy.md](tapin_philosophy.md), and the rules that hold
at every horizon are [tapin_principles.md](tapin_principles.md).

**Targets marked *proposed* are lines to judge against, not forecasts.** They are set for real at
the bridge's Jan 31 checkpoint, with the reason in the planning log.

## The horizons

### H0. The GTA 6 window (now to Dec 1)

- **Aim.** The year's biggest gaming moment, told better than anyone our size tells it.
- **Plan.** The bridge's wave 133: the countdown, three long videos, launch moments, launch week.
- **Signpost, Dec 1** (the bridge's): GTA 6 organic views and long-form hours from Oct 28 to Nov 30
  against every other subject.
- **This page adds** two readings on the same day: did the covers and footage of
  [look_and_feel.md](look_and_feel.md) land (no repeated frames, no other team's footage), and did
  the operator's morning stay near 15 minutes ([logistics_plan.md](logistics_plan.md))?

### H1. The bridge (Dec 1 to 2027-02-01)

- **Aim.** Both paths at once, by the bridge's lanes.
- **Signposts** (the bridge's weekly targets, summed to Jan 31): about 79 organic subscribers from
  the videos and about 85 long-form hours. By late January, about 900 organic views a day.
- **Jan 31.** The honest status goes in the planning log, and this page's proposed targets become
  real ones.

### H2. Under the new bar (2027-02 to 2027-06)

**Aim.** A library that earns hours while the operator sleeps, and Shorts that bring subscribers
rather than views alone.
- **Long-form becomes the spine.** Search-led guides and explainers on the marquee games and
  cards, chaptered, each with Shorts cut from it (decision 77). The bridge's math already favours
  long-form for hours and subscribers.
- **Series that recur.** A fight week, a launch countdown, a season preview, a monthly "what
  changed". A series teaches the viewer when to come back (philosophy 5).
- **Both worlds keep their marquee nights** (decision 85). The day's mix still follows organic
  views per video (decision 80).
- **Proposed signposts, 2027-06-30:**
  - 300 organic subscribers in total;
  - 400 long-form hours in the trailing 12 months;
  - 2,000 organic views a day;
  - a first month with money from outside YouTube above the month's spend.

### H3. The fork (2027-07 to 2027-12)

**Aim.** Commit to the path that is working. Which path is decided by the numbers at 2027-06-30,
by the branches below.
- **Proposed signposts, 2027-12-31:** 1,000 organic subscribers (the bar's first half) and the
  chosen path at half its bar on a trailing basis.
- **If YPP is reached.** Income goes back into what viewers see and hear: better footage capture,
  the voice, the operator's time. Spending rises only from income (P5).

### H4. After 2027

Written as questions for the operator, not as plans:
- **A brand or a channel?** TapIn on YouTube, Facebook Reels and TikTok as one voice
  (decision 82's crossposts), or YouTube first and everything else a mirror?
- **A second channel?** Only after TapIn passes its first bar. moneywise stays parked until then
  (decision 41).
- **The engine as a product?** Content OS for other creators, or its intelligence layer alone
  ([positioning.md](positioning.md), [desktop_app.md](desktop_app.md)). TapIn is the proving
  ground either way.
- **The operator on camera, or on the controller?** The most original footage a gaming channel has
  is its own play (P10). How much of TapIn becomes the operator's own capture?

## The branches

Each one is a condition, then what changes. A branch taken becomes a numbered decision.

| when | if | then |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 1 | GTA 6 long-form earned hours | long-form becomes the weekly default for marquee games (the bridge's rule) |
| Dec 1 | it did not | long-form returns to the Sunday Week in Review only, and the hours lane changes approach |
| Any week | a Short breaks out (100k+ organic views) | the breakout playbook below, within 24 hours |
| Any lane | it misses its target twice | it changes approach or gives its share to a lane that is ahead (the bridge's B10.5) |
| Jan 31 | neither path is near its line | the path with the better views-to-subscribers rate gets two thirds of the work; Shorts stay as discovery |
| 2027-06-30 | organic subscribers < 150 **and** long-form hours < 200 | a format change, not more of the same: fewer, bigger videos, a series, or the operator's own capture. The operator is asked again what TapIn is (decision 85's identity is re-asked, not assumed) |
| 2027-06-30 | one world (gaming or sports) earns over three quarters of organic views per video for three months | ask the operator whether "equally" still holds; the mix already follows the views (decision 80) |
| Any time | a policy strike or a reused-content rejection | [policy_incident_runbook.md](policy_incident_runbook.md), then an audit against P2 and P10 |

### The breakout playbook (no money involved)

1. Within 24 hours, a follow-up Short on the same subject with the next fact or the next date.
2. Within a week, a long video on the subject, with the breakout's chapters.
3. Pin a comment that asks for the viewer's call. Answer the first replies.
4. Put the breakout and its follow-ups in their franchise playlist, at the top.
5. Record what made it travel in the planning log: the subject, the first second, the cover, the
   hour it went out.
6. **No paid boost.** Paid reach does not count and does not change organic reach (decision 82).

## Stop and ask

These are not stop orders. They are the signals that should make the operator stop and decide on
purpose rather than drift:
- **The morning stops being 15 minutes** for a month, and the time goes to fixing the machine
  rather than to taste.
- **Growth seems to need breaking P1, P2, P3 or P10.** The answer is already written: grow another
  way (P12).
- **Spending creeps back.** A cost appears in `py -m scripts.ops cost-tower` that no decision
  named.
- **The work stops being fun to judge.** If the operator's 1-5 ratings drift to "fine" for a month,
  the topics have gone small again (decision 81's finding).
- **Six months with neither path moving** (2027-06-30's branch).

## The operator's time, over the horizons

- **H0-H1.** About 15 minutes a day, plus a long-form script a week and the monthly checks. Launch
  week is about 30 minutes a morning.
- **H2.** The machine handles footage rotation, covers and repeats without being asked
  ([look_and_feel.md](look_and_feel.md) L1-L8). The operator's time goes to taste: ratings,
  long-form scripts, and capturing their own gameplay.
- **H3+.** The time the operator spends is decided as part of the fork, not left to happen.
