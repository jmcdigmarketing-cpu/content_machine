Data deletion

To have the information {{APP}} holds about your account deleted:

  1. Remove the app's access in the platform's settings: for Google at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for TikTok and Instagram in the connected or third-party apps section of your account's settings.
  2. Email {{EMAIL}} with the subject "Delete my data" and the account's name or handle.
  3. Everything stored for that account, including its sign-in tokens and statistics, is deleted within 30 days, and the deletion is confirmed by email.
