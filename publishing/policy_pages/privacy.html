Privacy Policy

Effective {{UPDATED}}.

This policy explains what {{APP}} ("the app"), operated by {{NAME}}, accesses when it is connected to a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account, how that information is used, and how to have it deleted. The app connects only to accounts its operator owns or manages; it has no public sign-up.

Information the app accesses

YouTube (Google). The channel's name and ID; its uploads and their titles, descriptions, privacy settings and publish times; YouTube Analytics for those videos (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate, subscribers gained, estimated revenue); and comments on the channel's own videos, to find the questions viewers ask. The app uploads videos and thumbnails, adds videos to the channel's playlists, and changes a video's privacy setting or scheduled publish time.

TikTok. The account's basic profile (its ID, display name and avatar); the ability to send a video to the account or its drafts; and statistics for the account's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares).

Instagram (Meta). The professional account's ID and username; the ability to publish Reels to it; and insights for its own posts (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves).

The app does not access direct messages, contacts, followers' profiles, payment details, or any account that has not connected it.

How the information is used

Only to publish the operator's videos and to measure how they perform, so that later topics, video lengths and posting times can be chosen better. It is not used for advertising, it is not used to profile individual viewers, and it is never sold.

Where it is stored

On the operator's own computer, in a local database and local files. Sign-in tokens are kept in a local folder that is never published, uploaded or shared. No copy is kept on a server.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, with one exception. To draft a script, the app sends a prompt to an AI text-generation provider. A prompt can include the topic, public facts gathered about it, the text of questions viewers asked in public comments, and the titles and view counts of the account's own best-performing videos. Prompts never include sign-in tokens, and never include anything that identifies a viewer. Information may also be disclosed where the law requires it.

Retention

Statistics are kept while the account is connected, for as long as they help measure how the videos perform. Sign-in tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the account is disconnected.

Deleting your data

You can revoke the app's access at any time in the platform's own settings: for Google at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for TikTok and Instagram in the connected or third-party apps section of the account's settings. To have stored information deleted, follow the steps on the data deletion page; it is deleted within 30 days and the deletion is confirmed by email.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it with a YouTube account you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country, and does not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Information stays on the operator's computer, sign-in tokens are kept out of any shared or published location, and access to each platform is limited to what the features above need.
